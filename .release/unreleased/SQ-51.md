ref: SQ-51
title: "Worktree sweep deletes a finished ticket's tree at close even when it holds gitignored build output, reads git status four at a time, no longer blocks on a tracked symlink, and runs detached at SessionEnd"
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]
---

Fixes four defects found in 5.3.2. (1) Classification asked about untracked and ignored files before the ticket, so every finished tree (.next, .env.local, .devstack, test-results) sat 7 days as untracked_recent and 14 more in quarantine; the new ticket_closed_settled reason removes a done, archived, removed, or released ticket's tree whose HEAD is on the integration branch, in refs/sidequest/<ref> (not for released), or at its base, while untracked files that are not ignored and gitignored nested repositories still quarantine. integrate, groomClose, done, release and remove now reclaim at close. (2) git status ran on every tree at once under one 120 s cap; it now runs four at a time with a 60 s per-tree timeout, and the sweep reports how many timed out. (3) The 5.3.2 build kept any tree holding a symlink, so a repository that tracks one (index.md -> INDEX.md) could never be deleted; only an untracked link or one resolving outside the tree refuses deletion now. (4) SessionEnd ran the sweep inside its 10 s hook and was cancelled on every exit; it now starts a detached worker and returns at once, and bounded sweeps take closed-ticket trees first. New worktreeBudgetMaxCount (default 100) and worktreeBudgetMaxBytes (default 200 GiB) board config refuse isolated dispatch over budget and name the oldest deletable trees; worktrees sweep --yes ends with per-reason counts and the wall time.
