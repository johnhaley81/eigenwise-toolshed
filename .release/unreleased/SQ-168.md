---
ref: SQ-168
title: Quartermaster says when Eigenwise releases past the fork's upstream base
bump: patch
plugins:
  - quartermaster
---

On a machine that installs Toolshed from a fork, the prompt and Stop hooks now show a one-time "Toolshed fork sync due" reminder per upstream version when Eigenwise's marketplace version is above the base recorded in the fork's `.claude-plugin/fork-base.json`. A fork without that file, or an unreachable upstream, stays silent.
